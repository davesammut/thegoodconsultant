The book

The Good Consultant

A book about the craft of technology judgement — written, chapter by chapter, in public.

It isn't a technical manual. It's a collection of principles, approaches, and stories about the judgement behind good technology decisions — the kind that don't show up in a framework or a certification. Reading it now means watching it get written.

Story → Observation → Principle → Approach → Reflection

Every chapter follows this rhythm, whatever it's about.
