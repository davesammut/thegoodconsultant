How I work

Understand. Question. Decide.

Judgement only earns trust when it's grounded in evidence and delivered without ego. This is how I get there.

01

Understand what's really being decided

Separate the presenting question from the real one, and surface the constraints, incentives, and assumptions no one has said out loud.

02

Look at the evidence, not the narrative

Read the architecture, meet the team, and test the roadmap against what the system actually does — not what the slide deck says it does.

03

Give a straight, evidenced view

A clear opinion, not a menu of options with no owner. Where I'm uncertain, I say so — and why.

04

Leave you better equipped to decide

Hand over the reasoning behind the recommendation, not just the recommendation, so you can make the next ten decisions without me.

About

Curious enough to keep learning. Experienced enough to say what I actually think.

I'm Dave Sammut. For twenty-five years I've been the person brought in when a system, a team, or a decision needs to be understood properly before anyone commits real money to it.

I'm drawn to the moment before a decision is made — where a founder is choosing an architecture, an investor is trying to understand technical risk, or a CTO is working out whether the plan will actually survive contact with scale. My job is to make that moment clearer, not longer.

I still spend time with the code, the architecture and the people building it. It keeps me connected to reality and makes for better conversations in the boardroom.
