Thinking

A notebook, not a blog.

I write when I notice something worth thinking about. Patterns that keep appearing. Questions I find myself asking more than once. Tensions without obvious answers. Occasionally, something that changes how I think about a subject I thought I understood.

Some notes eventually become talks or chapters in The Good Consultant. Others remain exactly what they started as: an observation worth writing down. This is where the thinking is still allowed to move.

About

Curious enough to keep learning. Experienced enough to say what I actually think.

I've spent more than twenty-five years in technology, starting close to the code and gradually finding myself in conversations about architecture, organisations, strategy, and the decisions that connect them.

I've worked with engineers, technology leaders, and executives across different organisations and industries. I still value being able to move between the detail and the wider context — the engine room and the boardroom.

I still spend time with the code, the architecture, and the people building it. It keeps me connected to what's actually happening, not just what the deck says.

Along the way I've accumulated plenty of opinions, changed quite a few of them, spoken at conferences, and started writing a book about the craft of consulting.
